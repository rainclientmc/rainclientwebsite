In game

See it in game.

Every image here is a real screenshot of Rain Client running in Minecraft 1.21.11, only cropped or resized for the page.

Two layouts, one menu

Press Right Shift in game. Use the Panel layout with search, favourites and descriptions, or switch to Dropdown for one column per category.

A HUD you keep on

Small, readable elements you can drag, resize and snap anywhere in the HUD editor. Status Bars redraws your hearts, armor and hunger in Rain colours, as rounded bars, or leaves them vanilla.

WatermarkArraylistKeystrokesPotion HUDArmor HUDStatus BarsCompass

Skies that move

Custom Sky has 16 animated skyboxes, from sunsets and storms to galaxies and energy plasma, or you can load your own image.

Cosmetics

Wings, halos, capes, auras and more. They follow your body as you move and emote, and they are drawn on your screen only.

CS2-style knives

Swords become 3D knives with classic finishes like Fade, Doppler and Marble Fade. Press inspect to play an inspect animation in your hand.

Cars

Boats turn into ten different cars, with rolling wheels, glowing lights, a dashboard with a rev counter and exhaust flames. It is all visual and client side.

Emotes

Pick an emote from the wheel and the camera swings to third person. Each emote can play a song from your own music files.

Features

Small details, everywhere.

Panel or dropdown

Switch between a searchable panel and classic category columns. Both open with Right Shift.

HUD editor

Drag, resize and align HUD elements over your real game, with a grid, undo and redo, and saved layouts.

Rain Status Bars

Hearts, armor and hunger keep their usual shapes and layout, redrawn in Rain pink and blue.

Made for DonutSMP

A region map, chunk and region HUD, spawner tools, a shop price notebook and coordinate helpers.

Name and Skin Protect

Hide your name on your own screen for recordings, and show a different skin on yourself that only you can see.

Profiles

Save setups as profiles and share them with a friend as one line of text that starts with RAIN1:.
