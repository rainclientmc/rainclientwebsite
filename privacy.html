Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Rain Client has no Rain account and no Rain server. The client does not send your name, email address, IP address or any other personal information to us.

2. What Stays on Your Computer

Your settings, profiles, HUD layouts, waypoints and notes are saved in your Minecraft folder, under .minecraft/config/rain-client. Deleting that folder removes them.

3. Services the Client Contacts

Some features talk to other services directly from your computer, only when you use them:

  • Microsoft and Mojang, for signing in to accounts in the account manager, looking up skins (Skin Protect) and loading capes.
  • The iTunes Search API and LRCLIB, for album art and lyrics in the Spotify HUD.
  • The Discord app on your computer, for Discord Rich Presence.

Those services handle data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

4. This Website

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may collect basic usage data under its own privacy policy. The Discord and TikTok links take you to external platforms with their own terms.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us in our official Discord server.
